    [Normal] Authoritative source inventory
    [Normal] The evidence corpus is built from named, citable public sources. CMS publishes standardized denial and non-affirmation reason statements used by Medicare review contractors, which gives the triage agent a controlled vocabulary rather than free-text classification. CMS publishes public-use Medicare claims files containing non-identifiable claim-specific information, and specifically created Medicare Claims Synthetic Public Use Files (SynPUFs) so developers can work with realistic Medicare claims structures while protecting beneficiary privacy. CMS and Medicare publicly document the multi-level appeal workflow. Publicly available payer medical-policy documents are used, but frozen and versioned for the hackathon — the system never scrapes payer portals live. CMS distinguishes public-use files from identifiable data and states that public-use files do not contain PHI or PII requiring a data-use agreement, which is the clean data story cited in the demo.
 >>>[Normal] Table 8.1 — Evidence corpus source inventory. Each source is named, tiered, and frozen or versioned for the hackathon window.
    [Normal] Terminology discipline  Use six terms precisely and never interchangeably: synthetic (generated case), public-use (officially released non-identifiable dataset), de-identified (transformed per a de-identification standard), retrieved (external source pulled in), verified (human or deterministic check), and model-generated (AI output). The provenance architecture already distinguishes these categories; the discipline is to make the terminology visible in the UI and the Devpost copy.
    [Normal] 9.  Provenance & Citation Architecture
    [Normal] Provenance is elevated from a backend detail to a core product feature. Every important claim in an appeal carries a provenance card the judge can open with one click, which directly attacks the hallucination concern that sinks most LLM-in-healthcare submissions. The provenance record is a structured object, not a free-text citation, so it is machine-checkable by the Quality Review agent and human-checkable by the judge in seconds. This is the single most cost-effective credibility upgrade in the entire plan: it costs roughly one day of engineering (Day 5) and lifts the Innovation and Architecture scores simultaneously.
